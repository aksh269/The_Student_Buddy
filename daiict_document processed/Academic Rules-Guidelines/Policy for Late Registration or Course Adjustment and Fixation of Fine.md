Late Registration or Course Adjustment policy and Fixation of Fine

1. Late registration (only for the purpose of fee payment) is allowed from the date of
commencement of classes till the last date of late registration as specified in the Academic
Calendar.

2. Alteration in Registration beyond the time frame specified in the Academic Calendar for
Add/Drop Course adjustment and Drop Course adjustment is not allowed.

3. However, the amendment in Registration beyond the time limit is allowed only in special case
subject to the payment of fine (after following appropriate procedure) prescribed as under:
