MTech Project/Thesis Policy

Master of Technology in Information and Communication Technology – MTech is a fulltime two-year (four
semesters) program. The document contains the description of the project and thesis courses structure for
the competition of MTech degree for the MTech 2022 batch onwards.
As a part of graduation requirements, a student has to do multiple projects, starting from second semester
of the program. This document provides a broad guideline with respect to project and thesis.

In the curriculum effective from 2022 batch, the following components have been introduced:


Minor Project-I (Second Semester)

Major Project-I (Summer Semester)

Major Project-I continuation (Third Semester)

Major Project-II or Industrial Internship (Fourth Semester)

Thesis: A student can choose to do major project-II on campus or undertake an internship in industry and
R&D establishment. If the student undertakes on-campus major project-II, it must be a continuation of
Major Project-I. Upon successful completion of Major Project-II, student is awarded a thesis certificate.

Terms and Conditions for Minor and Major Project

The MTech Coordinator will arrange a presentation with the supports of various research groups of
the institute in order to share their research activities with the students, before start of the second
semester.

A student has to identify a faculty mentor for pursuing MTech project in the area of his/her interest.

The work can be carried out individually or in a group (nor more than two students) under the
supervision of faculty. It is expected that a faculty member supervises not more than Two M.Tech.
students as Primary supervisor and not more than Two students as Co-supervisor.

The student should undertake a research problem of interest, identify the research challenges, and
develop/devise techniques to address the research problem. It is advisable that a student must have
sufficient foundational knowledge (e.g. course work in respective specializations) in the area of
interest of his/her project/thesis work.


MTech coordinator will communicate with the students and faculty about the schedule, evaluation
committee and grades related matters. MTech coordinator will take help of Specialization
coordinator as and when required.

MTech coordinator will form evaluation committees in consultations with PG Convenor for the
minor/major projects. The evaluation committee consists of Supervisor(s), a domain expert and a
member of the respective specialization of the student’s project area.

Each Project has to be presented by the corresponding student(s) before an evaluation committee
as per the announced schedule. Prior to the presentation schedule, students must submit the
project report to the evaluation committee, as least a week before.

Based on the work accomplished in the project and the presentation, the student’s project will be
assessed by 50% weightage to the grade received from the supervisor(s) and 50% weightage to the
grade received from other members of the evaluation committee. The MTech coordinator will
tabulate the final grades of the students based on the supervisor(s) and members’ weightage.

Industrial Internship: A student may opt for an industrial internship through Placement Office, which has
to be decided before the start of their fourth semester.
1. The nature of industrial internship should technical with a combination of development and research
flavor.
2. Student has to identify an on-campus faculty mentor in addition to their off-campus mentor. The
faculty mentor will monitor the students’ performance periodically and will be the contact point with
the off-campus mentor.
3. All students who will pursue internships, will be required to make their presentation before an
evaluation committee as per the announced schedule.
4. The assessment of student’s performance will be decided by the on-campus faculty supervisor (on-
campus supervisor - 50% weightage, off-campus supervisor -50% weightage).
5. If a student opts for internship though his/her own arrangements, then the internship should be in a
research establishment or in an organization with repute. The evaluation/assessment criteria will be
same as industrial internship.

Expectation and Deliverable of Minor/Major Project:

A student should consider the following metrics in his/her project exploration:

Technical problem with research and development blend in mind.

Substantial literature study in the area of exploration.

Devise/develop novel methodology.

Analyze/Experiment/Simulate the results.

Summarize the project outcomes as a technical report.
