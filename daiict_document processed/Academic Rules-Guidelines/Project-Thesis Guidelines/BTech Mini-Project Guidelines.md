B.Tech Mini Project Guidelines

B.Tech Mini Project (BMP) is a course offered to the B.Tech(ICT) and B.Tech(ICT+CS) students
with effect from 2016-17 batch. The credit structure of this course is 0-1-4-3. This course is
considered under the Free/Open Elective category, and is offered to encourage supervised project
work.

Terms and conditions of BMP registration:
 Student needs to have a CPI of 7.5 or more to register for BMP.
 Student has to do BMP on-campus and under the supervision of a member of faculty.
 Student is allowed to register for at most one BMP in one semester.
 Student is allowed to register for a maximum of two BMP (the combined credit for BMP
should not exceed 6) during the entire program.
 A BMP course will not be considered as an overload.
 Student is allowed to register for BMP in the semester in which (s)he is not registering for
any Audit course.
 Students can do a BMP in a group of not more than 2 students.
 A faculty member can supervise 3 BMP in a semester.
 A student can register for BMP in Semester 6 onwards.
