## Page 13

After clicking Confirm and Next in any step, if you want to edit a slot or subject before pressing the final
Submit button, you must jump to the specific step and click the EDIT button. This will open the edit mode for
that step, allowing you to change your slot or subject. Once edited, click Confirm and Next again.
Please note: if you make changes to any step in between, all the following steps will be reset. You will then
need to reselect and complete all the subsequent steps again.
