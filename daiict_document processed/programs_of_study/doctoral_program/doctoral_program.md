# Overview

**Dhirubhai Ambani University** (Formerly DA-IICT) offers an intense Doctoral of Philosophy (Ph.D.) program in ICT and allied disciplines, Mathematical and Computational Sciences and Humanities and Social Sciences. Admission to the Ph.D. program is possible through the following channel: Ph.D. (Regular & Part-Time) The details of the admissions process for the above channel can be found [**here**](https://www.daiict.ac.in/admissions#tab-3)

# Main Content

**Dhirubhai Ambani University** (Formerly DA-IICT) offers an intense Doctoral of Philosophy (Ph.D.) program in ICT and allied disciplines, Mathematical and Computational Sciences and Humanities and Social Sciences. Admission to the Ph.D. program is possible through the following channel:

Ph.D. (Regular & Part-Time)

The details of the admissions process for the above channel can be found [**here**](https://www.daiict.ac.in/admissions#tab-3)

Financial assistance is provided to all Ph.D. (Regular) students who have enrolled as full-time students in the form of Teaching Assistants. The details of pedagogy, curriculum, courses and related things can be found in the Ph.D. Program page as given under:

- [Ph.D.](https://www.daiict.ac.in/phd)
