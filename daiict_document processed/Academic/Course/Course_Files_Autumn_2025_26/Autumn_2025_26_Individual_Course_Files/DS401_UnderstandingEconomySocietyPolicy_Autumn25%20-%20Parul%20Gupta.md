DS 4XX
Understanding Economy, Society and Policy: A Data-Driven Approach
Autumn 2025

Instructor: Dr. Parul Gupta

Course Placement: DS 4XX is a technical elective/open elective for Second Year (Semester 3) MSc
Data Science/MSc IT students.
Course Format: 3 hours of lectures and 2 hours of lab each week (3-0-2-4)

Pre-requisites: None

Course Content: This course introduces students to foundational economic concepts in macroeconomics
and development economics. The interconnections and complementarities between economics, society
and government policies will be discussed. Students will be encouraged to recognise and appreciate the
two-way relationship between economics and policy-making. The course will focus on economic
reasoning through the analysis and interpretation of real-world data. Lectures will be supplemented with
guided empirical analysis of real-world economic problems.

Course Material (Selected):

1. Doing Economics, CORE Team
E-book available online: https://www.core-econ.org/doing-economics/book/text/0-3-
contents.html

2. Economy, Society and Public Policy, CORE Team
E-book available online: https://www.core-econ.org/espp/book/text/0-3-contents.html

3. Basic Economics: A Citizen's Guide to the Economy, Thomas Sowell (5th edition). ISBN:
9780465081455

Data-based exercises will be completed using MS Excel/Google Sheets with the help of publicly
available datasets.

Method of Assessment:
Data exercises (30 %)
Mid-term exam (30 %)
Group project and presentation (40 %)
Plagiarism on any assessment component will result in a failing grade for the course.

Course Outcomes: On successfully completing the course, students will have a basic familiarity with
economic reasoning and global socio-economic concerns. They will become aware of publicly accessible
sources to obtain real-world macroeconomic data. They will be able to analyse and interpret data and
communicate their findings in written and verbal formats. In doing so, they will be able to enhance their
writing, presentation and analytical skills.
