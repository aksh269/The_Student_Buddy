Course Title: Optimization
Course Code: IE402
Credit Structure(L-T-P-Cr): 3-0-2-4
Prerequisites: Fundamental understanding of Linear Algebra, Matrix Theory.
Suggested Textbook/references: Operations Research by HA Taha 8th/9th Edition
     Linear Programming by Howard Karloff
     Combinatorial Optimisation by C. H. PAPADIMITRIOU
  K. STEIGLITZ
Evaluation Scheme
In Semester Exams (30%)
End Semester Exam (50%)
Assignments and Homework (20%)
Course Outcome:
After the completion of course:
The students will be able to understand the application and working knowledge of
optimization in selected areas.
The students will develop an insight and understanding with respect to formulation of
optimization problems and designing the optimal solutions with applications in various
domain and engineering applications.
The students will get acquainted with applications of optimization problems for some
practical system model and few relevant work being carried out.
