MC224 Parallel and distributed algorithms
• Course placement: Parallel and distributed algorithms is a core
course for second year students of B.Tech MNC program.
• Pre-requisite: Data Structures and Design and Analysis of Algo-
rithms.
• Course format: It is 3 hours lecture and 1 hour tutorial every week.
• Course content: The aim of the course is introducing the main
concepts and methods in parallel and distributed algorithm design,
together with performance evaluation and comparison with sequential
algorithms. A wide range of topics are discussed in depth, including
lists and trees, searching and sorting, graphs, and arithmetic compu-
tations.
• Text book:
1. V. Kumar, A Grama, A. Gupta, and G. Karypis, Introduction to
Parallel Computing: Design and Analysis of Algorithms.
2. Joseph Jaja, An Introduction to Parallel Algorithms, Addison
Wesley.
3. J. Suomela, Distributed Algorithms.
• Assessment method: Scribe notes, two in-semester examinations
and a ﬁnal examination.
• Course Outcomes: Students who complete the course should have
the ability to do the following:
– Able to tell the parallel world from the distributed world.
– Apply techniques and methods presented along the course aiming
to design eﬃcient parallel and distributed algorithms.

Topics covered:
• Parallel Algorithms: Modelling Parallel Computations - Multipro-
