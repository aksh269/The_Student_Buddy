## Page 1

Industrial Internship Report Format

1. The report should be submitted as a pdf file, studentID.pdf, to the Industrial
Internship Coordinator at DA-IICT (cii@daiict.ac.in) by 13th July.
2. The first page should consist of information regarding the student, industry,
supervisor and duration of the internship.
3. Students should write in brief about:
a. the industry unit where the internship was performed;
b. ICT related issues analysed during the internship; and
c. learning and experience about - how ICT is used in carrying out industrial
operations and - how what is learnt in the classroom/labs is related to how
things work in industrial environment, etc.
4. The report (all inclusive) should not exceed four A4 size pages. The text should
be in 12pt font.
