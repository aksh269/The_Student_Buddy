Industrial Internship
Summer-05
Orientation
Ashish N Jadhav

## Page 2

Vision Statement
n To expose the undergraduate students to
the working environment in Industry
n It is expected that this exposure to the
work environment would equip the
student to make a better choice about
his career after graduation

## Page 3

Overview
n The Industrial internship is a required part of
the academic activity for the Second Year BTech
students
n This internship is expected to expose students
to the industry culture, industry ways of doing
things, how to carry out group activity, inter-
personal dynamics and behavior, real life ICT
situations having learnt various aspects of ICT in
the class and the labs

## Page 4

Duration
n The Industrial internship is of 6-8 weeks
duration
n The Industrial Internship each summer should
end by 31st July
n 2003 batch students can not register for
summer courses in summer-2005 semester
n It is preferable that the internship begins from
1st June after the completion of Re-examination

## Page 5

Important Entities and their
Responsibilities
n Student – to undergo internship in the assigned
industry
n CCE&IR – to establish initial formal
communication with industry to place students
for internship and maintain internship related
records
n On-site Supervisor – To allocate work, supervise
the internship and assist in the evaluation
process
n Industry Internship Co-ordinator – To formulate
internship related documents, evaluate and
grade the students

## Page 6

Requirements (1 of 2)
n An on-site supervisor is to be assigned
who would look after the internship work
of the students and interact (if required)
with DA-IICT
n The supervisor would allocate work to be
carried out by the students

## Page 7

Requirements (2 of 2)
n The supervisor would assist in the
evaluation of the students at the end of
the Internship
n Student is supposed to complete the
work assigned to him and submit an
“Industrial Internship Report” to the
Industrial Internship Co-ordinator at DA-
IICT

## Page 8

Expectations from Students (1
of 4)
The student is expected to
i. Study the industry unit in detail
ii. Analyze ICT related issues
iii.
Learn how ICT is used in carrying out
operation and how what is learnt in
the classroom/ labs is related to how
things work in industrial environment, etc.

## Page 9

Expectations from Students (2
of 4)
n The student is expected to take care of
his travel, stay and food expenses
n The student is expected not to take any
leave other than those announced by the
organization and follow the timings of the
organization

## Page 10

Expectations from Students (3
of 4)
n The student should report immediately to
the supervisor in the event of exceptional
circumstances like illness
n Student is expected to follow the rules
and regulations of the organization as
briefed by the supervisor

## Page 11

Expectations from Students (4
of 4)
n Student is expected to complete the work
assigned to him by the supervisor in the
stipulated timeframe
n Student is supposed to submit a short
“Industrial Internship Report” in the
prescribed format to the Industrial
Internship Co-ordinator at DA-IICT by
the announced date

## Page 12

Evaluation
n Student is supposed to submit a short
“Industrial Internship Report” in the
prescribed format to the Industrial
Internship Co-ordinator at DA-IICT
n Industrial Internship Co-ordinator at DA-
IICT to award a ‘Pass/Not Pass’ grade to
each student after receiving feedback
from the on-site supervisor
