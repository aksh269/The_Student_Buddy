## **General Guidelines for I year B.Tech Rural Internship Students** 

Come with open minds.  Take the internship seriously to benefit from the internship. 

_Internship Objectives_ : This is a rural internship for our students to experience life in rural India, understand it, and learn from it.  A second objective is to identify opportunities for improving the life – doing it differently, systematically, using technology, and using information and communication technology.   A third objective is to contribute to the objectives and activities of your host organization. 

1. Appoint a spokesperson for your team.  Coordinate with your team members to arrive at your destination within a day of each other. 

2. Your internship kit should contain the name/address of NGO where initial training will take place (as per your assignment), contact information about the supervisor, a journal, these guidelines, safety/behavior norms, and emergency phone numbers. 

3. You should also take along thin towels, a torch, water container for carrying drinking water, and a lota for going out for other bodily needs.  It would be wise to take something to use for bedding, sheets, and mosquito net if you have space.  Carry a good map if you can get it. 

4. An important aspect of rural internship is the participatory approach.  Everyone participates in solving a problem or making a decision: you, your team members, the host organization, villagers, and others.   Ask your supervisor to introduce you to village elders and leaders.  Periodically chat with them to get their views.  They will have much to teach you. 

5. Here is a process that will help you understand, clarify, and explain your ideas for improvements:  a) First Observe and Understand;  b) Describe how it is done today;   c) Explore, experiment, and describe how it would be done differently with your improvements;  d) List limitations of your improvement  e) Describe obstacles you are likely to face while introducing your improvements 

6. Plan: Create a daily schedule, and weekly/monthly schedule.   Every morning check schedule and edit if necessary. 

7. Take the initiative.  If you do not know what to do, ask and discuss with your supervisor. 

8. _Self-monitoring and Self-evaluation_ : Every evening, make entries in your journal, and cross-check with schedule if goals were achieved.  If not, revise plan of action for next day to ensure improvement.   If yes, give yourselves a nice pat on the back  

9. In your journal, write down your observations on rural life, think of ideas on how to improve current functions for more efficiency (in short, moving from A, not to just A+ but A+++) and jot them down, evaluate your own performance in a given situation and jot it down.   Write down your experiences during the day evaluating your learning, your contribution, and any other relevant or fun stuff.   You can really enjoy this internship if you get into it. 

10. At the end of the internship, submit your journal along with a 5-page report to the rural internship coordinator for a pass/fail grade.
