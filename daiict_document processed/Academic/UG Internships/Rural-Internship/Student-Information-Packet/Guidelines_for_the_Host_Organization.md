## **Guidelines for the Host Organization** 

## **Why Rural Internship?** 

For our students the rural internship is to experience social and economic aspects of rural life in India, and understand and learn from it.  We also want them to identify opportunities for improving the quality of rural life – doing it differently, systematically, using technology, including information and communication technology. 

From past experience, it is ideal to assign a group of 2-5 students per village.  Such groups allow students to support each other and make the experience enjoyable for everyone. 

## **Choosing a Task** 

The best way to achieve this, we believe, is by giving the students specific tasks and responsibilities that involves useful interactions with the village community and village life. Please choose a task well so that it benefits the student as well as the host organization. 

Examples of tasks that help achieve the objectives of the Rural Internship 

Working with the rural development organization to support organization, planning and execution of: 

- Health camps and services 

- Building check dams 

- Education 

- Enabling information sharing and communications related to water management, education, energy conservation, environment, health, agriculture and preservation of biodiversity 

Stuffing envelopes, typing reports, distributing mail are examples of tasks that do not help meet the objectives of the Rural Internship. 

## **Capabilities of Student Interns** 

The internship is for our first year B. Tech. students.  They are not technical experts and this expertise should not be expected of them. 

Please keep in mind that they are with you to learn and are paying for this internship. 

## **Support from the Host Organization** 

From  experience,  we  have  found  that  coordination  between  DA-IICT  and  the  host organization is streamlined if a single person is appointed by the host organization to act as a coordinator.  Such a person can introduce the students to village life, plan their integration within the organization, assign them tasks, supervise and mentor them, and evaluate their work at the end of the internship. 

## **Everyday Necessities** 

Except for an initial orientation period, it would be much better if the students spend the majority of this internship as paying guests in village homes to really experience rural life.
