## **Appropriate Behavior / Etiquette / Safety Norms as guests in a village** 

1. Follow your host family’s timetable/schedule for wake-up in the morning, breakfast and other routine and maintain their discipline. 

2. Courtesy is the mother of all virtues.  Display this in your behavior at all times. Appreciate the food they share with you without being supercilious about it.  They know much more about life and their local environment than we do.  It is just that they know different things.  Don’t come with pre-conditioned minds.  Don’t try to impose your values on them.  Treat them as your teachers and figure out a way to learn from them. 

3. Learn a few phrases in the local language, for example, greetings, asking for directions, stating the name of your host organization. 

4. Don’t stare at their women-folk and embarrass them (they are not used to urban behaviors). 

5. Adapt to the rural life and enjoy a taste of this different culture, without complaining. You may have various types of toilet adjustments (outdoor field? Bathe by the well?). Be prepared for such eventualities. 

6. Remember again courtesy demands that you display exemplary behavior in the way you interact with each other, without silly fights, or flirtatious public behaviour. 

7. Walking alone at night, swimming in unknown places where disallowed – ask your hosts if it is safe. 

8. At all times, remain aware of safety rules and customs prevalent in your local area and abide by them.  Follow local rules and customs.  Do not dive into unknown bodies of water.   Do not walk alone at night even if you are going out to the bathroom. 

9. Do not drink, fight, quarrel – find alternate ways.  If you are confronted by hostile people: a) walk or run away – safety is more important than your pride;  b) call out the name of your host organization or village elders in the local language; 

**IMPORTANT:** Please obtain local medical emergency contact information (phone number and address of clinic, doctor, pharmacy) from your supervisor at the host organization as soon as you arrive for your rural internship.
