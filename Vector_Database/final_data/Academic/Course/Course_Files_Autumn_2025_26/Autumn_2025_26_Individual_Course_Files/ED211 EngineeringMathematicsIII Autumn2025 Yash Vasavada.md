ED211: Engineering Mathematics III
Course Placement a core course for the second year BTech EVD students
Course Format three Lecture hours and two Lab hours per week
Course Content The course ED211 introduces the students to the various mathematical
and engineering techniques related to time and frequency analysis and synthesis, Fourier
Transformation, Fourier Series, etc.
The course demonstrates application of elegant
mathematical principles to practical engineering design concepts underlying many algo-
rithms implemented in today’s electronics and VLSI systems. The students gain hands-
on skills on these topics by performing software laboratory experiments throughout the
semester. The specific topics covered are continuous-time Fourier Transform, Fourier
Series, effect of sampling, discrete time Fourier Transform, DFT, Z transform, convolu-
tion and linear time/shift-invariant systems, design of digital filters, eigenanalysis and
its application to OFDM signaling, etc.
Course Description:

 Discrete Time Signals. Sampling and its effects
 Analysis and Processing of Discrete Time Signals. Discrete Time Fourier Transform

 Z transformation.
Z transformation and its relation to Laplace transform and
DTFT, Poles and Zeros, Region of Convergence.
 Digital Systems. Design and analysis of digital filters.

Course Outcomes this course introduces the students to the engineering knowledge (P1)
of the mathematics and engineering fundamentals related to signal analysis and signal
processing by linear systems. The students engage in the analysis (P2) and design (P3)
of the basic modules of a linear discrete-time and continuous-time system. As part of the
Lab/Tutorials, the students conduct investigations of the complex design and analysis
problems (P4) and make use the modern tools (P5) such as Matlab and Python. Through
the Lab/Tutorial that is team-based and require a formal presentation by the students,
they acquire the team-work (P9) and the technical communication (P10) skills.
