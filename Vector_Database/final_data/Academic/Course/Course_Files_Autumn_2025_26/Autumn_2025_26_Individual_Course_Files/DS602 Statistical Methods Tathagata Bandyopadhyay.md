## Page 1

Statistical methods –Data Science

Course Placement: It’s a core course for students in Masters in Data Science Programme.
Course format: Three 50 minutes’ lectures every week.
Lab: 2 hours every week

Course Content:
Data summarization: Data types, Data visualization and summarization with the help of tables and
descriptive measures.
Probability: Statistical interpretation of probability, Bayes’ theorem and its applications.
Standard probability models: Binomial, Poisson, Uniform, Exponential and Normal, Data generation from
different models using Monte Carlo method.
Sampling: Concepts of population and sample, Sampling distributions of sample mean and sample
proportion, Central limit theorems, Illustration of sampling distribution using Monte Carlo simulation.
statistical inference
Statistical estimation: Point and interval estimation, Methods of estimation (method of moments &
maximum likelihood method)
Statistical testing of hypotheses: Null and alternative hypotheses, Significance testing using p-value,
Standard parametric and non-parametric tests
Regression Analysis: Linear and Logistic regression
Time Series Analysis: Components of time series, Determination of Trend and Seasonal components
Special topics:
Resampling technique: Bootstrap
For all topics course notes will be provided by the instructor. In addition, the following books may be
used as ready references.
References:
1. Statistics for Business & Economics – Anderson et al., Cengage Learning (2018)
2. Introduction to Data Science -- Rafael A. Irizarry (CRC Press but freely available in
https://leanpub.com/datasciencebook)
3. An introduction to probability theory and its application (Volume -1) —William Feller (Wiley
series in probability and statistics)
4. Statistical Inference – George Casella and Roger L. Berger (Cengage Learning India)
5. An introduction to the Bootstrap—Bradley Efron and Robert J. Tibshirani (Chapman Hall/CRC
Press)

6. Introductory Statistics with R – Peter Daalgard, Springer (2008)

Assessment method: Quizzes, project presentation, and an end-term examination
Course Outcomes: After completion of this course, students will
●
Learn the fundamental concepts and the basic tools of Data Analytics
●
Learn the implementation of the analytics tools using R
●
Acquire the skill to analyze data in a systematic and logical manner to answer important
research questions arising in different disciplines, especially in the field of agriculture
●
Learn how to attach a measure of uncertainty to the inference drawn from data
●
Get a feel for data driven decision making
