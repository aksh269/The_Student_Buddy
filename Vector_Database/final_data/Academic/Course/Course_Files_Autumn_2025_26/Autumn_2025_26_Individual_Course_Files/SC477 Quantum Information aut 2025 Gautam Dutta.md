SC477: Quantum Information Theory
Course content
DA-IICT, B.Tech,
Objective:
This 4th year B.Techs course deals with the essential physical and mathematical processes involved
in Quantum computation and quantum communication. is an introduction to the emerging field of
Quantum Computers. Students are expected to know Quantum Mechanics but it is not mandatory.
The phenomenon of entangled states and its use in controlled operations are introduced. With this
basic tools from the physics of Quantum Mechanics the course intends to prepare the students to
understand some of the important algorithms in quantum computation, quantum Information and
Cryptography.
Prerequisite: Quantum Mechanics, Linear Algebra, Probability
1. Quantum Theory
(i) Qubits
(ii) Quantum Evolution
(iii) Measurement Projective and POVM
(iv) Composite system
(v) Entanglement, Teleportation, Quantum Key
2. Noisy Quantum Theory
(i) The density operator
(ii) Composite quantum system
(iii) Partial trace and reduced density operator
(iv) Schmidt decomposition and Purification
(v) EPR paradox and Bell inequalities.
3. Quantum Evolution
(i) Evolution of a pure state
(ii) Noisy system
(iii) Noisy system evolution
(iv) Bit flip and phase flip channel
(v) Depolarizing channel
(vi) Amplitude damping
(vii) Phase damping
4. Quantum Error Correction
(i) Three qubit bit flip code
(ii) Three qubit phase flip code
