ENVIRONMENTAL STUDIES

Course Placement:    B.Tech 2nd year MNC Students. Core course
Course Format:  2 Lectures per week
Course Code: MC 226
Course Name: Environmental Studies
Course Objective:

 Students will integrate knowledge from multiple disciplines representing physical and life
sciences perspectives, political and economic perspectives, social and cultural perspectives
on humans’ interactions with environments.

Students will contribute to and facilitate interdisciplinary research and problem solving,
through independent and collaborative work.
Learning Outcomes: After completing   Environmental Studies, students will be able to:

Articulate the interconnected and interdisciplinary nature of environmental studies;

Demonstrate an integrative approach to environmental issues with a focus on sustainability;

Understand and evaluate the global scale of environmental problems.

Reflect critically on their roles, responsibilities, and identities as citizens, consumers and
environmental actors in a complex, interconnected world.
Course Content.
•
Module 1: History and Multidisciplinary Nature of Environmental Studies.
•
Module 2: Ecosystems
•
Module 3: Natural Resources
•
Module 4: Biodiversity and Conservation
•
Module 5: Environmental Pollution
•
Module 6: Social Issues and Environment
•
Module 7: Human Population and Environment
•
Module 8: Sustainable Development and Green Practices
•
Module 10: Field Work /Project Work/ Paper Presentations
