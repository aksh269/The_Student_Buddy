# PC731-%20Ethnography%20and%20Applications

Course PC 731 Ethnography and Applications 3-0-0-3

Course Instructor - Vishvajit Pandya

Course Objectives :Contemporary Communication Design informed by multi-media
technologies are  meant to be immersive, engaging and meaningful experiences. The
method of Ethnography which derives from the discipline of Anthropology  teaches students
to understand cultures, communication and social activities at large  through, immersive
exposure and participant-observation. This makes it  particularly relevant to the discipline of
Communication Design.  Ethnographic analysis provides powerful tools to understand how
communication practices, tools and systems inform and impact social relations and cultural
practices.

Course Content :

The module introduces the foundations and basic methods of ethnography derived from the
fields of visual anthropology and social and cultural anthropology. It will look at several
applications of these disciplines to current communication practices. Students would be
expected to undertake fieldwork assignments and related applied activities such as
collection of empirical data, qualitative data, contextual inquiry and usability testing that
provides the foundations for user-experience design and visual design. The objective of the
module is to provide the student the intellectual tools to develop research capacity for the
final design projects they would have to undertake.

Students will be also required to read and be prepared for in-class presentations and
discussions of their readings and how these speak to their field experiences.

Evaluation

## Page 2

There will be continuous evaluation for this module, and include, in-class readings and
presentations, field work and the writing and presentation of field reports and research
papers.

Course outcome

At the end of the module, students will be able to acquire a deeper understanding of user-
research beyond the usual field survey or AI driven analytics. They will deploy their
ethnographic research insights to develop better user-profiles and journeys and therefore
design more ethically, effectively and responsibly.
