Your supervisor at the host organization will be asked to evaluate you
at the end of the internship. Your grade partially depends on this
evaluation. More importantly, you can use the report as feedback for
yourself.

The evaluation form will be sent to your supervisor and will be based on
the following criteria:

1.  Did the student show up on time?

2.  Was the student's attendance and timeliness satisfactory?

3.  What tasks did you assign to the student?

4.  Did the student to listen to and follow directions with minimum
    supervision?

5.  Did the student complete assigned Tasks satisfactorily?

6.  Were you satisfied with student's contribution?

7.  What was the overall attitude of the student?

8.  Did the student show initiative and adaptability?

9.  Did the student experience the rural life fully?

10. What comments did you hear from villagers about this student?

11. What comments did you hear from your team members about this
    student?

12. What did you observe about this student's interactions with
    villagers?

13. What did you observe about this student's interactions with your
    team members?

14. Comment on the Learning or Growth that the student has experienced
    during the internship

15. Would you want this student as a part of your team?

16. Based on your experience with this particular student, would you
    invite student interns next year?

17. Additional Comments
