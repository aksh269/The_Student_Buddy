## **Report on your Internship** 

You need to submit a 5-page typed report along with your journal by 10-August. 

Your report must address the following topics and questions.   Realize that you need to write a report and not an enumerated set of answers to an enumerated set of questions. 

Organize it to show your own experiences in the best possible way.  Make it flow.  Make it unique.   Write it as if you going to publish it as a story or a poem or an essay. 

1. What were your expectations before you started the internship? 

2. Describe how these expectations were met or not met. 

3. How could you have better prepared for the internship? 

4. What were your impressions about rural people and life before you started the internship? 

5. How has the internship experience changed your impressions about rural people and life? 

6. Describe the village and the area where you stayed, what your daily routine was. 

7. What tasks were assigned to you by your host organization?  Were they satisfied with your contribution?  Were you satisfied with your contribution? 

8. Describe 1-3 of your own ideas about improving or changing rural life with or without technology. 

9. Out of these ideas, what is one idea you would try to implement if you were to stay longer? Describe how. 

10. Describe one experience that you enjoyed the most. 

11. Describe the most humorous thing that happened to you. 

12. Describe the most difficult situation or challenge you faced and how you handled it.  What did you learn from it? 

13. Describe one thing that you will enjoy narrating to your friends and grandchildren.
