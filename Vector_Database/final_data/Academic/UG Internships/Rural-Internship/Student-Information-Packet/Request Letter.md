June 24, 2026 

## Chairperson 

## Dear Shree Chairperson: 

Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information and Communication Technology, Gandhinagar, has provided for Rural Internship as an essential part of every undergraduate student's study programme.  This provision is made so that students learning Information and Communication Technology get some idea of the social and economic realities of rural India.  Under this programme, DA-IICT sends students to various organisations known for their innovative approach to rural development for a stay of about a month during the month of May.  The expenses for the stay, food, local travel, etc. are borne by the students.  The responsibility of the host organisation includes providing an initial briefing, day-to-day supervision and guidance, help in finding lodging and boarding, possibly as a paying guest with a village family, and evaluation of his or her work.  In the past, our experience has been very encouraging, and the organizations to which students were attached found the partnership fruitful. 

In this context, the name of your organisation has been suggested to us as a possible host for some of our students.  The dates for this year's Rural Internship will be 2 May to 31 May. 

I am writing to request you to let us know if you would be willing to accept a small group of students for their Rural Internship, and if you would be able to provide them with the necessary supervision for making their stay a meaningful experience. 

I will greatly appreciate if you let us have your response at the earliest, but certainly before the 10th April. Please indicate the number of students you would be willing to accept.  The finalisation process of matching students with host organizations will be completed by the 20th of April so that students can plan their travel. 

I would like to request you to kindly suggest the name of the person, who will be given the responsibility of supervising the work of the students.  This will facilitate the coordination between our two organizations. 

With warm regards, 

Prof. Dilip Soni Coordinator Rural Internship - Rural_Internship_Coordinator@DA IICT.ORG Tel: (079) 30510 619 Fax: (079) 30520 010
