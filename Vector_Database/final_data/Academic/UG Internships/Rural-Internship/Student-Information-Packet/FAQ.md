## **Frequently Asked Questions** 

## **1. What is the duration of the rural internship?** 

- One month. 

## **2. What if I have to come back for re-examination?** 

Note down the dates for re-examination.  Notify your supervisor.   Appear for the reexamination and report back to the host organization.   Do not use this as an excuse to reduce the duration for the internship. 

## **3. When do I submit my journal and report?** 

Last date for submission of journals is 10-August. 

## **4. Do I have to come back to DA-IICT to submit my journal and report?** 

   - No.  You need to submit your internship journals and reports after you come back to the institute next semester. 

**5. How will I be evaluated?** 

Your evaluation will depend on an evaluation by your supervisor at the host organization, and your journal and report.  Refer to separate documents about what is expected. 

## **6. Is it possible for me to fail the rural internship?** 

   - Yes.  If you do not fulfill the internship objectives adequately or do not spend the required amount of time, you will fail.  If you fail, you need to repeat it with your juniors. 

**7. What do I do when the host organization has not made arrangements for accommodation?** 

First this should not happen.  The spokesperson for your team should have notified the host organization about when you are arriving.   Talk to your supervisor at the host organization.   Arrange for a temporary accommodation at Mandir/Masjid/Church or dharamshala, or the railway platform. 

**8. What if I run out of money?** 

Always keep some money for emergency situations.  Arrangements have been made with the host organizations to provide for your accommodation, major meals, and local travel. Some organizations may also make arrangements for breakfast and tea.  Some may not. You will need to adjust.  So carry sufficient amount of money with you for snacks, tea, telephone calls, etc. 

**9. What if I do not like the food?** 

   - Adjust .  Develop a taste for it.  Buy fruits and snacks to keep with when you get hungry. 

**10. What if I get lost?** 

Don’t .  If you separate from your group, tell at least person where you are going. Know the general area around where you are staying.  Agree upon a designated place to meet and get together.  Go to the organization office or the home of a village elder. Contact your supervisor. 

## **11. What if I get into a fight with a villager?** 

Don’t .   Walk/Run away – your safety is more important than your pride.  Take deep breaths to calm down.  Speak out the name of the host organization, supervisor, or village elder in the local language. 

## **12. What if my group is split into smaller groups?** 

- This is likely to happen.  Assignment to one organization is no guarantee that you will be all together in a single village.  Adjust. 

## **13. What if I get sick?** 

Take medicines for common ailments with you.  Contact your supervisor at the host organization.  Use the emergency information you collected when you arrived.
