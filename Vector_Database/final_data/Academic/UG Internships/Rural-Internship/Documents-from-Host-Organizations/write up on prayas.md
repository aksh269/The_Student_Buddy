**Prayas** (Endeavour) is a voluntary organisation working for social, political and economic development in Chittorgarh District of southern Rajasthan. 

Prayas was originally established in Delhi by Dr. Narendra Gupta with like-minded associates and registered under the Societies Act. In 1979, Narendra started work in Devgarh – one of the remotest  and most backward tribal areas of Rajasthan, by setting up a dispensary in the crumbling palace building of Devgarh and then in a room in a Jain temple in the village. 

Today Prayas is working in 32 panchayats of Devgarh Tehsil, 7 Panchayats of Chittorgarh and has also undertaken work in Dhariyawad area of Udaipur district and parts of Madhya Pradesh. 

Over twenty five years Prayas has been characterised by creative efforts and experimental, openended attitudes. 

## **Objectives of Prayas** 

- To provide an enabling environment for creating opportunities for social and economic development of poor and deprived people. 

- To formulate alternative definitions of knowledge and development for community. 

- To work for protection of social, economic and political rights of dalits and poor people. 

- To work towards meeting immediate needs of the society. 

- To work for sensitization of community at social level to remove gender discrimination. 

## **Areas of working** 

To meet the above objectives Prayas is working on following issues: 

1. Reduction in class and caste-based atrocities 

2. Addressing land alienation 

3. Women’s empowerment and reduction in gender disparities 

4. Right to health care 

5. Child Rights 

6. Right to education 

7. Right to livelihood 

8. Natural resource management 

9. Rural housing
