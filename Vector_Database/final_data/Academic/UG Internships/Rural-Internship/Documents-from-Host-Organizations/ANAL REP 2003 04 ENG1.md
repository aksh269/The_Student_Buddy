## _ANNUAL ACTIVITY REPORT 2003 – 2004_ 

##  _ESTABLISHMENT:_ 

_Sarvangin Gram Vikas Sanstha, is a Non Government Voluntary Organization which is working since 1992 in rural villages and its surrounding areas. The organization is registered under the Society Registration Act of 1960 Guj./667/S.k. and  it  is  also  registered  under  Trust  Act  Registration  Act  F/657/S.k.  dated 26.02.1993._ 

##  _MAIN OBJECTIVE:_ 

## _**UNITY – STRUGGLE – DEVELOPMENT**_ 

_So keeping in mind the above mentioned objective organization is working for different kind of survival activities mentioned below:-_ 

- _1) To create Women Self Help Groups for Savings, Internal Lending & Earn Livelihood_ 

- _2) HIV / AIDS Awareness Programs / Different Programs on Health_ 

- _3) Enterprenuership Development Programs for becoming a successful Businessman_ 

- _4) Different programs for Women and Child Development_ 

- _5) Programs on Panchayati Raj and Duties towards the same_ 

- _6) Programs to Sustain the Dalits, Exposed, Poor and Affected people of the rural area_ 

- _7) Programs for Protection and Propaganda on Prohibition_ 

- _8) To give voluntary services during the period of Natural Calamities and Communal Riots_ 

- _9) Programs on Environmental Development and Protection_ 

- _10) Programs for Water Shade activities_ 

- _11) Awareness Programs for Poor Disables of rural areas_ 

- _12) Arranging Legal Aid Programs_ 

- _13) Programs on Energy Savings_ 

- _14) To operate Other Survival Activities on voluntary basis_ 

_So,  the  organization  is  working  since  last  11  years  for  the  above  mentioned activities with a huge platform of its surrounding villages._
