Summer Research Internships

Objective
To help undergraduates make an informed decision whether to pursue a career in
industry or one in academia. Summer Research Internship (SRI) program is a step
towards this.

To expose students to the research culture.

Scope
Intern must learn to explore in an independent environment how to approach
o Theoretical research
o Experimental research
o Conceptual development of some product

Duration
DA-IICT requires that the duration of Summer Research Internship is of 6-8
weeks. It may start in the 1st week of May 2005 but must end by 31st July 2005.

Expectation from the Intern

o Identifies a research area
o Carries out detailed literature review
o Analyzes current problems of that area and identify gaps
o Explores possible approaches to fill these gaps
o Submits a weekly one page report to the Advisor/Mentor indicating the
sequential development of work
o Submits a Research Report (13-15 pages) at the end to Advisor/Mentor.
o Identifies a possible B Tech project at the end of the internship.

Expectation from the Institute/Organization

o Assign an Advisor/Mentor for the Intern
o Inform the Coordinator – Summer Research Internship course about
Intern’s deliverables, start and end dates of internship, location of work etc.
o Identify/Define the area/problem on which Intern is expected to work
o Provide logistic and academic/intellectual support to the Intern

o Send feedback (form will be sent separately to Adviser/Mentor during the

o Send the Research Report submitted by the Intern to the Coordinator –

Evaluation

o On technical/scientific content of the Research Report. This shall be done
at DA-IICT.

be sent by the Advisor/Mentor of the Intern (form will be sent separately to
Adviser/Mentor during the course) to the Coordinator – Summer Research
Internship.
