# Summer Research intern Guidelines

Source: `Z:/minor project/daiict_documents raw/Academic/UG Internships/Guidlines/Summer Research intern Guidelines.pdf`

## Page 1

SumResearchI2007 (SRI 2007)
Important Instructions:
1. Please note that Summer Research Internship (SRI) is different than Industrial Internship. SRI
2007 is for BTech 2004 batch. All the information about SRI 2007 is available in the Academic
folder/UG Internships/Research-Internship/SRI-2007 folder in the DAIICT university folder
sharing system.
2. This is primarily meant for getting an exposure to research. You can do it at DAIICT or any other
reputed research led university in India or abroad or any research lab in the industry. Most of
the places in the industry research labs are competitive and you should have good academic
background to get it. You have to approach the industry research labs in a professional manner.
You can apply at their web site online or you can also contact any research staff in the lab by
sending an email and your CV if you like his/her research work. Remember to keep a
professional attitude in your email and never write a language which is written in chat text. Be
very careful about it.  The same approach has to be followed while writing to any other scientist
in a university. Never use a free email to send an email. Always use an organization email to
send an official email. If you use gmail then you must use an organizational envelope  on the top
of it. So the reader will see that your email has arrived from an organization. If you do not get a
reply immediately then do not resend the email until you waited for sometime. After signing an
agreement with the mentor you need to get an approval from coordinator-summer research
internship by submitting the prescribed form (hard copy) to Ranjit Chauhan (Faculty Block-2) by
April 25, 2007. Please also include with the agreement your hard copy CV, your hard copy grade
report showing all the courses that you have done with grades. There are two agreement forms
one for internal use and one for external use. For the approval we may ask any additional
document.
3. Please try to get SRI at a place where you can further do the BTech project. In other words, SRI
2007 is an opportunity for you to start working on your possible BTP project which will be done
in 4th year. So if you choose a mentor and a topic for SRI 2007 and your BTP same you can
really contribute something leading to a research paper or some other tangible output.  Most of
the people will like this both in industry and in academia.
4. You need to type your report in latex and a style file has been provided to you for this in the
SRI-2007 folder.
5. You must write the report in your own words. Do not copy sentences etc from any published
material or online sources on Internet. Failure to do so will be considered as plagiarism. Penalty
for plagiarism is a failing grade and a possible expulsion from the DAIICT. Your submitted report
may be checked for plagiarism with tools such as turnitin. You should include proper references
at the end of your report. Get yourself some education for plagiarism issues at
http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plagiarism
6. The SRI2007 can start as early as May 7, 2007 and can go up to 31 July 2007. The minimum
duration is 6-8 weeks. You need to submit a final report in latex format (two column) and pdf
format (two column) by an email to csri@daiict.ac.in by 31 July 2007 mid night. Please note that
any email send after this firm deadline will not be accepted. You will be responsible for an email
lost. To be on the safe side you can also submit a hard copy of the report to  Mr. Ranjit Chauhan
(Faculty Block 2) by the evening of 31 July 2007. Any report not submitted in latex format will
be rejected.
7. There will be poster presentation workshop towards the last week of July or the the first week of
August. More information about this will be circulated to you in due time.
8. While applying for outside SRI do not include the name of any professor for reference letters
unless you have discussed it with him/her. Approach the professor  for reference  letter with
your CV and grade report. It is better if you have done any course or project with him/her. Do
not include the name of your friend as a reference letter writer.
9. You are welcome to send an email at csri@daiict.ac.in for any additional questions regarding
SRI2007.
