Dear  Sir/Madam

It gives us great pleasure to write to you about Dhirubhai Ambani Institute of Information
and Communication Technology (DA-IICT), Gandhinagar, Gujarat. DA-IICT started
functioning in August 2001 by offering 4-year UG Programme, i.e., B. Tech. in
Information and Communication Technology (ICT).  DA-IICT is a UGC recognized
university. The ICT is a unique discipline offered for the first time by an Institute in the
country and we believe it is very timely for all of us.

The complete programme is structured in such a way that our 3rd year B. Tech. students
have
to
undergo
a
Summer
Research
Internship
in
a
research
focused
institute/industry/organization for 6-8 weeks. By that time our students have completed
six semesters in the institute. They are well acquainted with the fundamentals of
Electronics and Communications, Computers and Networks, and Software Engineering
alongwith some specialized electives of their own choice.

We feel that to help undergraduates make an informed decision whether to pursue a career
in industry or one in academia we must encourage them to get exposed to appropriate
research environment. Summer Research Internship (SRI) course is a step towards this.

We would be grateful if your organization could accommodate our student as Intern for
this duration in the summer. At the earliest it may start from 1st week of May 2005.

Thanking for your cooperation and looking forward to a positive response at the earliest.

Regards
